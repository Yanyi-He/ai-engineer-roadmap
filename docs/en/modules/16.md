## 16.1 RL and dynamic environments

`elective` · Explain mechanisms, limits and choices

Prerequisites: [03.3 ML tasks and generalization](../modules/03.md#unit-03-3), [03.4 Neural networks and optimization](../modules/03.md#unit-03-4)

### Knowledge outline

- States/actions/transitions/rewards and partial observability
- Bellman, value/policy methods and actor-critic
- Online/offline, model-free/model-based and imitation
- Exploration, credit, shift and safety
- Simulation metrics and real-environment evaluation

### Reading and scope

[Hugging Face Deep RL Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/deep-rl-course/unit0/introduction) — Complete free RL theory/practice course for elective foundations.

MDPs, values, policies, gradients and actor-critic.

### Build task

Specify an MDP and reproduce a simple policy.

### Self-check

1. Explain how the Bellman equation, value functions and policies relate, and the basic structure of actor-critic methods.
2. An agent performs well in simulation but fails with a different random seed or environment parameters. What could be the cause?
3. Compare the data needs and risks of online RL, offline RL and imitation learning, with a suitable scenario for each.

## 16.2 World models and planning

`elective` · Explain mechanisms, limits and choices

Prerequisites: [16.1 RL and dynamic environments](../modules/16.md#unit-16-1), [04.2 Transformer computation](../modules/04.md#unit-04-2)

### Knowledge outline

- Latent states, dynamics, observations and actions
- Video generation, interactive simulation and decision tasks
- Learning→imagined rollout→planning/policy improvement
- Uncertainty, compounding error, OOD and control
- Research questions around Dreamer/JEPA and applications

### Reading and scope

[DreamerV3](https://github.com/danijar/dreamerv3) — Author world-model implementation for research questions and experiments.

Author code/paper and training/evaluation pipeline.

### Build task

Explain one world-model pipeline and evaluation target.

### Self-check

1. What is a world model? Explain Dreamer’s loop of learning a model, imagining rollouts and improving the policy.
2. Errors compound when a world model is used for long-horizon planning. Why, and what mitigates it?
3. Compare video-generation models with world models for decision-making in their goals and evaluation, and explain the idea behind JEPA.

## 16.3 Robotics software and simulation

`elective` · Implement a small system and verify behavior

Prerequisites: [01.6 Environment, Linux and collaboration](../modules/01.md#unit-01-6), [03.1 Linear algebra and numerical basics](../modules/03.md#unit-03-1)

### Knowledge outline

- Frames, kinematics, sensors and control loops
- ROS2 nodes, topics/services/actions and recording
- MuJoCo/Isaac Lab, reset and task definition
- Randomization, sim-to-real and safety
- Reproducible simulation, versions and task metrics

### Reading and scope

[MuJoCo Documentation](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/overview.html) — Official physics simulation, models and Python interfaces.

Models, simulation, control and Python basics.

### Build task

Run a simulation and record observations/actions/success.

### Self-check

1. Explain how ROS2 nodes, topics, services and actions differ and what kind of communication each suits.
2. A policy succeeds in simulation but fails on the real robot. Analyze the sim-to-real causes and what domain randomization does.
3. Define a grasping task in MuJoCo or Isaac Lab: observations, actions, reward, reset and success criteria.

## 16.4 Imitation, VLA and robot data

`elective` · Explain mechanisms, limits and choices

Prerequisites: [16.3 Robotics software and simulation](../modules/16.md#unit-16-3), [03.5 PyTorch training engineering](../modules/03.md#unit-03-5), [05.6 Vision, documents and multimodal input](../modules/05.md#unit-05-6)

### Knowledge outline

- Teleoperation, demos and multimodal action data
- Behavior cloning, ACT, diffusion and action chunks
- VLM→VLA, language policies and actions
- Data/task/robot shifts, generalization and recovery
- Offline/simulation/hardware evidence

### Reading and scope

[LeRobot Documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/index) — Public robot-data, policy, training and evaluation guides.

Datasets, policies, evaluation and hardware adaptation.

### Build task

Inspect a LeRobot dataset and explain training/deployment interfaces.

### Self-check

1. Explain compounding error in behavior cloning and how ACT’s action chunking mitigates it.
2. How is a VLA model built from a VLM? How are language instructions, visual input and action outputs connected?
3. Design robot data collection: teleoperation, multi-task coverage and failure-recovery demonstrations, and how to evaluate generalization.
