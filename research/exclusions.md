# Selection and exclusions

Included: application products, agent platforms/harnesses, customer-facing engineering delivery and task-specific application optimization. Titles alone are insufficient.

Excluded: pure research/model-training roles without application delivery, sales-led solutions jobs, IT-administration-led roles, mismatched employer identity, closed pages without a usable description, responsibilities-only snippets and exact body/location duplicates. The final three scope removals are recorded in [jd-exclusions-v3.json](jd-exclusions-v3.json).

Historical or syndicated descriptions can inform capability analysis when their status is labeled. They are not counted as confirmed active openings. Canada/US cohorts require an explicit eligible location; remote jobs without a country are not assigned by guesswork.
