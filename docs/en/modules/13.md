## 13.3 Batching, scheduling and serving frameworks

`advanced` · Implement a small system and verify behavior

Prerequisites: [13.2 Prefill, decode and KV cache](../modules/13.md#unit-13-2), [02.4 Caches, queues and workers](../modules/02.md#unit-02-4)

3. Explain what chunked prefill and prefill/decode disaggregation (DistServe, Mooncake) solve, and what they cost to deploy.

## 13.7 Resource accounting, roofline and optimization choices

`core` · Diagnose failures, compare and repair

Prerequisites: [03.1 Linear algebra and numerical basics](../modules/03.md#unit-03-1), [04.2 Transformer computation](../modules/04.md#unit-04-2)

### Knowledge outline

- Account separately for weights, precision, gradients, optimizer, activations, KV and runtime
- Compute for prefill/decode, forward/backward, batch and sequence length
- FLOP/s, bandwidth, arithmetic intensity, roofline and compute/memory bounds
- Upper bounds, usable capacity, caching/fragmentation and concurrency budgets
- Estimate, profile, explain discrepancies and apply one targeted optimization
- Training scaling, inference compute and business value have different extrapolation limits

### Reading and scope

[CS336 Lecture 2: PyTorch and resource accounting](https://github.com/stanford-cs336/lectures/blob/main/lecture_02.py) — Readable code lecture for memory, FLOPs and arithmetic intensity.

Lecture 2 memory/FLOPs/arithmetic intensity and Lecture 10 inference workloads; estimate before measuring.

### Practical evidence

Create a memory/compute budget for one model, measure a small run, explain errors and choose the next optimization.

### Self-check

1. Explain the roofline model and arithmetic intensity. How do you tell whether an operator is compute-bound or memory-bound?
2. Your estimated inference throughput is three times the measured value. List factors that could explain the gap and how you would confirm them with a profiler.
3. Estimate the FLOPs per training token and per generated token for a 7B model (about 6N and 2N), and state where these estimates break down.
